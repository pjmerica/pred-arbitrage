Guaranteed arb — buy Yes on one platform + No on the other. Total cost < $1 = locked profit regardless of outcome. Net gap subtracts estimated round-trip fees (Kalshi ~2%, Polymarket ~2%, PredictIt ~12%). Stake ratio = how to split your bankroll so both legs pay out equally. Sorted by guaranteed first, then soonest settlement date. Suspicious = gap >40pp, likely a data or matching artifact — hidden by default.
