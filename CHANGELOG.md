- `[3124d93]` matcher: relative strike-tolerance floor kills 8 fake XRP guaranteed arbs — user reported the board wasn't finding real arbs anymore. Cause: `match_threshold_pairs` used `tolerance = max(2.0, strike * 0.02)`, whose `2.0` term is an ABSOLUTE $2.00 window. That's fine for BTC ($40k strike → $800 tol) but for a low-priced asset it is wider than the entire strike ladder: XRP trades ~$2-4 in $0.20 rungs, so Kalshi "XRP above $2.00" matched Polymarket "XRP reach $4.00" (distance $2.00 ≤ tolerance $2.00) and every rung between. The 8 resulting pairs are genuinely different questions, and because a $2.00 market is far likelier to resolve YES than a $4.00 one, the price spread looked like locked profit — they took 8 of the top 10 guaranteed slots (13.6% at the top), burying the real arbs. Floor is now relative: `max(strike * 0.02, 0.01)`, so it scales with the strike instead of swallowing small ladders. Validation on 2026-07-05 data: threshold pairs 66 → 57, all 9 dropped pairs confirmed mismatched-strike; the 8 legit equal-strike crypto arbs (BTC/ETH/SOL) all survive, as does Kalshi's `$199,999.99` ≡ Polymarket's `$200,000` rounding drift the percentage tolerance exists to absorb.

## How to update this file

When you add a commit:

1. If the entry deserves its own line (most code commits do), add it
   under the dated section for today. Format:
   `` - `[hash]` <commit subject> — <one-sentence WHY> ``
2. Data-refresh commits from the workflow get NO entry. They're
   autonomous.
3. If you're in the middle of a multi-commit feature, add entries to
   the **Unreleased** section at the top and move them under the
   dated section when the feature ships.

Why dated sections instead of semver versions: this project has no
releases — it's a continuously-deployed dashboard. Date is the
only sensible group key.
